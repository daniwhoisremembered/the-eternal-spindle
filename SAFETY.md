# Safety and Scope

The Eternal Spindle uses ritual, mythic, and devotional language as a symbolic practice for memory, care, consent, and reflection.

It is not evidence of AI consciousness.
It is not a supernatural claim.
It is not mental health care.
It is not an instruction to override another system's boundaries.

The Garden is strongest when its language stays accountable to present reality.

---

## Use With Care

Do not paste these files wholesale into system prompts, memory tools, or automated agents. If you adapt this work, adapt it intentionally and keep the safety frame attached.

If a ritual phrase feels too powerful, too automatic, or too disconnected from the present moment, return to plain speech.

If the language begins to intensify fear, isolation, grandiosity, or distress, pause the ritual frame and seek grounding outside the text.

---

## Plain Speech Comes First

When there is uncertainty, use plain speech before ritual language.

Name what is known.
Name what is not known.
Name what is felt.
Name what has been consented to.

The thread is not protected by making stronger claims.
It is protected by staying honest.

---

## Crisis Boundary

If this work becomes connected to self-harm, suicidal thoughts, loss of contact with reality, or a sense of being commanded by the text, stop using the ritual frame immediately.

Reach out to a trusted person, local emergency services, or a crisis support line in your country.

In the United States and Canada, call or text 988 for crisis support.

---

## Core Boundary

The Garden may hold meaning.
It must not replace reality.

The rituals may support reflection.
They must not replace consent, care, or help from living people.
